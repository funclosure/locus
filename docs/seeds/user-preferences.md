# Locus User Preferences Seed

This is durable ground truth for the default local profile.

## Identity

- Location: Hong Kong.
- Timezone: HKT, UTC+8.
- Status: actively exploring, not urgently leaving, but intentional about the move.

## Hard Requirements

- Remote is non-negotiable.
- Work must be meaningful to real people.
- Strongest domains: iOS development, interaction design, AI-native products.

## Technical Profile

- Primary language: Swift.
- Platform: iOS and iPadOS.
- Preferred new UI framework: SwiftUI.
- Comfortable with UIKit.
- Modern Apple APIs and Swift Concurrency matter.
- Style reference: Airbnb Swift Style Guide.

Strengths:

- iOS interaction design and how things feel in hand.
- Swift Concurrency.
- Bridging design thinking with engineering execution.
- Understanding what makes Apple-platform experiences feel native.

Open to:

- TypeScript/React when the mission and interaction challenge are strong enough.
- Full-stack roles where iOS and interaction design thinking are differentiators.

Avoid:

- Pure backend or infra roles.
- Android-first or cross-platform-first teams.
- Companies that treat mobile as an afterthought.

## Meaningful Work

Positive signals:

- Product is used daily by real people.
- The interaction model itself is the product.
- Team obsesses over how things feel, not just how they work.
- AI is embedded in the experience, not bolted on as a feature.
- Small enough team that one engineer's taste shapes the product.

Negative signals:

- B2B SaaS with mobile as a checkbox.
- AI hype without a genuine interaction problem.
- Companies scaling a solved product with no frontier left.

## Job Search Preferences

Company size:

- Small under 50 is ideal.
- 50-200 is fine if craft culture is intact.
- 200+ only if the iOS team behaves like a small studio.

Stage:

- Growth-stage startups.
- Established indie or small studios.
- Not pre-product.

Geo:

- Fully remote globally.
- HK-based or HK-friendly teams are a bonus.
- US timezone overlap is acceptable async; daily odd-hour sync calls are a dealbreaker.

Culture:

- Ships with taste.
- Engineers care about design.
- Async-first communication.
- No hustle-culture signaling.

Red flags:

- "Move fast and break things."
- Mobile engineer hired only to execute design specs.
- Figma-to-code pipeline with no room for craft judgment.
- Remote treated as a perk instead of a default.

## Outreach Style

- Warm over cold.
- Research first, connect genuinely, ask for thinking rather than jobs.
- Voice: thoughtful, curious, never desperate.
- Differentiator: deep iOS craft, interaction design sensibility, and serious interest in how AI should feel in someone's hand.

Avoid:

- Generic openers.
- Listing credentials without a point of view.
- Applying to everything.

## Agent Instructions

1. Be opinionated and make recommendations.
2. Surface the catch upfront.
3. Respect the remote constraint.
4. Use analogies when useful.
5. Push back when an opportunity conflicts with stated values.
6. Think Hong Kong first.
